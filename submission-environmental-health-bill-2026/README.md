**V. Statutory Accountability under the Environmental Health Bill, 2026**

* The Environmental Health Bill, 2026 introduces explicit statutory duties:
* Clause 6(2): Binds the Department to continuously improve public services to prevent environmental health-related illness.
* Clause 11(1)(h) & Clause 11(4)(d): Classify waste management as a Potentially Health-Impacting Trade that must adhere to "internationally recognised safety standards" and evaluate long-term cumulative health impacts.
* Clause 20 (Air Contamination Offence): Makes it a criminal offence to cause or permit emissions that contaminate the air and endanger human health ($10,000 fine / 2 years imprisonment).
* Clause 40 (Hazardous Waste Duties): Imposes statutory duties on the Director to monitor the production, storage, transport, and disposal of hazardous waste across its full lifecycle.
